## How does the system work?

Skin keeps water in and a great deal of the outside world out. The outer layer is cells and fats. Under it, blood vessels, nerves, and immune cells handle temperature, touch, and repair. When the barrier is stripped — too much washing, a harsh product, a scratch — skin gets drier, itchier, and easier to irritate.

A changing mole, a wound that will not heal, a rash with fever, or swelling that spreads needs a clinician. This page is not a diagnosis of eczema or infection.

## Emotions and psychosomatics

Itch is worse when you are tired or on edge, and some people pick or scratch more in those weeks. Stress does not invent a rash, and a rash is not a moral failing. Poor sleep also slows the feeling of repair. Treating inflamed skin as “just anxiety” delays the bland care, or the prescription, that the barrier actually needs.

## Aromatherapy

Tea tree and lavender are among the oils dermatology clinics see as causes of allergic contact dermatitis, especially when they are used neat or often. Citrus oils can burn skin that then sees sun. Read [sensitization and phototoxicity](/blog/safety-sensitization-phototoxicity) and [tea tree limits](/blog/tea-tree-skin-antimicrobial-limits). There is no recipe for a rash that is already angry. Inhaling a mild scent avoids the barrier question and is the better default while skin is irritated.

**Intact-skin patch** — diluted topical, only on calm skin

- Lavender — 1 drop
- Stir into 1 teaspoon of a plain carrier oil
- Try a coin-sized patch on the inner forearm. Wait a day before using it anywhere else
- Do not use on broken skin, on the face, or before sun. Skip tea tree in this recipe. Stop at redness, itching, or burning.

## Daily care

- Use a bland moisturizer on dry skin, and do not add a new scent while a patch is already angry.
- Prefer lukewarm water over long, hot, soapy showers.
- Use sunscreen on skin you will expose. Do not put cold-pressed citrus oils on that skin.
- Rinse sweat if it sits under tight clothes.
- Sleep shows on skin because itch and repair both track the night.

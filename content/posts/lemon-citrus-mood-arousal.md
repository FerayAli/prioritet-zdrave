## Why this matters

Bright citrus scents are associated with cleanliness and energy. A modest number of human studies test whether **inhaled lemon or sweet orange oil** shifts mood, nausea, or physiological stress markers.

## What researchers did

RCTs and controlled studies use diffusion or personal inhalers with lemon (*Citrus limon*) or sweet orange oil during dental anxiety, office work, or postoperative settings, measuring anxiety scales, nausea, or mood profiles.

## Main findings

- Some trials report **lower anxiety or improved mood** with citrus inhalation versus control in procedural anxiety settings.
- Lemon aroma studies in **pregnancy-related nausea** show mixed but occasionally positive self-report outcomes with short exposure.
- Effects are typically **modest** and setting-specific; few long-term follow-ups exist.

## What this does not prove

Citrus essential oils can be **phototoxic** (especially cold-pressed bergamot and some lemon expressions) on skin in sunlight. Mood trials do not validate immune or “detox” marketing.

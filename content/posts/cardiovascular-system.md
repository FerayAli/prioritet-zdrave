## How does the system work?

The heart is a pump. Arteries carry blood outward, veins bring it back, and smaller vessels deliver oxygen to muscle, brain, and every other organ. Blood pressure is the force of that flow against artery walls. Resting heart rate and how you feel on a hill are different questions from how a room smells.

Chest pain or pressure, pain into the arm or jaw, sudden shortness of breath, fainting, or one-sided weakness is an emergency. Nothing here replaces blood-pressure medicine.

## Emotions and psychosomatics

A fright raises heart rate and blood pressure for a few minutes. That is a normal alarm, not a personality flaw, and it is not the same as hypertension. A life with no recovery can keep the nudge going. Feeling anxious about your heart can also make normal beats feel dangerous. Both deserve a calm explanation, and new or severe symptoms still deserve a clinician rather than a story that “stress caused it.”

## Aromatherapy

There is no appropriate recipe for circulation, cholesterol, or blood pressure on this page. Lavender shows up in anxiety and sleep reviews, which can be part of how an evening feels. That is not a heart treatment. Read [what those anxiety reviews measured](/blog/lavender-anxiety-what-reviews-say). Do not swallow oils, and do not stop a heart medicine because a blend claims to support circulation.

## Daily care

- Walk most days, including a little pace that makes conversation slightly harder.
- If you sit for work, stand or move for a few minutes each hour.
- Know your blood pressure when a clinician has asked you to.
- Build the evening so sleep can happen: dimmer light, caffeine earlier, a stopping point for work.
- Do not smoke. If that is the change in front of you, ask for help made for quitting rather than a scent.

## Essential oils and aromatherapy

Essential oils are concentrated plant extracts. They can irritate skin, cause allergic reactions, and interact with medications or conditions. Some oils are unsafe during pregnancy, breastfeeding, or for infants, pets, or people with asthma, epilepsy, or other sensitivities.

- **Ingestion** of essential oils is not recommended on this site unless a qualified professional has directed you to a specific, studied product.
- **Phototoxic** citrus oils can cause burns or pigmentation if applied to skin before sun exposure.
- Science posts summarize published research; they describe what *studies* tested, not a guarantee that your product will work the same way.

Always follow dilution guidance from reputable safety references and patch-test new topical blends.

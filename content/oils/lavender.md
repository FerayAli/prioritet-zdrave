Lavender is a floral oil (Lavandula angustifolia). In this book it is a component: recipes can name it, and this page is the short note for the oil itself.

## What people use it for

- Evening wind-down
- A familiar sleep-blend note
- A simple scent when stress is high

This is educational copy, not a recipe and not medical advice.

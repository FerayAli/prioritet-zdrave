Black pepper is a spicy oil (Piper nigrum). In this book it is a component: recipes can name it, and this page is the short note for the oil itself.

## What people use it for

- A warming spice note
- A small drop in digestion-leaning recipes

This is educational copy, not a recipe and not medical advice.

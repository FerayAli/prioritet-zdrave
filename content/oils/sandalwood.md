Sandalwood is a woody oil (Santalum spp.). In this book it is a component: recipes can name it, and this page is the short note for the oil itself.

## What people use it for

- A relaxing evening wood scent
- A hair-moisture recipe component

This is educational copy, not a recipe and not medical advice.

Coriander is a spicy oil (Coriandrum sativum). In this book it is a component: recipes can name it, and this page is the short note for the oil itself.

## What people use it for

- A spice-herb kitchen scent
- A clear-skin recipe component

This is educational copy, not a recipe and not medical advice.

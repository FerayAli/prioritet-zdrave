Cassia is a spicy oil (Cinnamomum cassia). In this book it is a component: recipes can name it, and this page is the short note for the oil itself.

## What people use it for

- A warm baking-spice aroma
- A bold note used sparingly

This is educational copy, not a recipe and not medical advice.

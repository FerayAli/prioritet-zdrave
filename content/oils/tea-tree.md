Tea tree is a camphoraceous oil (Melaleuca alternifolia). In this book it is a component: recipes can name it, and this page is the short note for the oil itself.

## What people use it for

- A skin, hair, and nail recipe component
- A clean, camphor room note

This is educational copy, not a recipe and not medical advice.

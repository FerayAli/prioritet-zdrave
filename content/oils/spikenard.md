Spikenard is a earthy oil (Nardostachys jatamansi). In this book it is a component: recipes can name it, and this page is the short note for the oil itself.

## What people use it for

- A calming earth scent
- A purifying skin recipe note

This is educational copy, not a recipe and not medical advice.

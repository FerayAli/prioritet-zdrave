Basil is a herbaceous oil (Ocimum basilicum). In this book it is a component: recipes can name it, and this page is the short note for the oil itself.

## What people use it for

- A study-time diffuser note
- A green accent in herb blends

This is educational copy, not a recipe and not medical advice.

Lime is a citrus oil (Citrus aurantifolia). In this book it is a component: recipes can name it, and this page is the short note for the oil itself.

## What people use it for

- A sharp citrus room scent
- A bright note in daytime blends

This is educational copy, not a recipe and not medical advice.

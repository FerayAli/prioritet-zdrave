Spearmint is a herbaceous oil (Mentha spicata). In this book it is a component: recipes can name it, and this page is the short note for the oil itself.

## What people use it for

- A gentler mint for digestion recipes
- A sweet-herb room scent

This is educational copy, not a recipe and not medical advice.

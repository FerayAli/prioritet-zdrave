Geranium is a floral oil (Pelargonium graveolens). In this book it is a component: recipes can name it, and this page is the short note for the oil itself.

## What people use it for

- A floral personal scent
- A skin-and-hair recipe component

This is educational copy, not a recipe and not medical advice.

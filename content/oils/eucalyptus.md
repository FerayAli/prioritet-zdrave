Eucalyptus is a camphoraceous oil (Eucalyptus radiata). In this book it is a component: recipes can name it, and this page is the short note for the oil itself.

## What people use it for

- A clear-breathing chest blend
- A tension-easing inhale

This is educational copy, not a recipe and not medical advice.

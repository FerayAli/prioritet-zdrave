Wild orange is a citrus oil (Citrus sinensis). In this book it is a component: recipes can name it, and this page is the short note for the oil itself.

## What people use it for

- A cold-weather citrus room scent
- A cheerful daytime blend note

This is educational copy, not a recipe and not medical advice.

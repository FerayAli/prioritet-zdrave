Ginger is a spicy oil (Zingiber officinale). In this book it is a component: recipes can name it, and this page is the short note for the oil itself.

## What people use it for

- Stomach discomfort recipes, often next to peppermint
- A warming travel or cold-day note

This is educational copy, not a recipe and not medical advice.

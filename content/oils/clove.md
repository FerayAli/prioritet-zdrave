Clove is a spicy oil (Eugenia caryophyllata). In this book it is a component: recipes can name it, and this page is the short note for the oil itself.

## What people use it for

- An alert, spicy room note
- A warming drop in winter blends

This is educational copy, not a recipe and not medical advice.

Marjoram is a herbaceous oil (Origanum majorana). In this book it is a component: recipes can name it, and this page is the short note for the oil itself.

## What people use it for

- A soothing herb massage note
- A quiet stress-time diffuser scent

This is educational copy, not a recipe and not medical advice.

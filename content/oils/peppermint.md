Peppermint is a camphoraceous oil (Mentha piperita). In this book it is a component: recipes can name it, and this page is the short note for the oil itself.

## What people use it for

- Digestive discomfort, often next to ginger
- A cooling inhale or temple note

This is educational copy, not a recipe and not medical advice.

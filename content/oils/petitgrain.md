Petitgrain is a woody oil (Citrus aurantium). In this book it is a component: recipes can name it, and this page is the short note for the oil itself.

## What people use it for

- A calming green-wood scent
- An evening blend next to lavender

This is educational copy, not a recipe and not medical advice.

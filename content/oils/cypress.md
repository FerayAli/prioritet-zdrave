Cypress is a woody oil (Cupressus sempervirens). In this book it is a component: recipes can name it, and this page is the short note for the oil itself.

## What people use it for

- A fresh wood room scent
- A pre-walk or exercise blend note

This is educational copy, not a recipe and not medical advice.
